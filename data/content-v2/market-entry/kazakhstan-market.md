# Kazakhstan Market Entry Plan

## Market Overview

Kazakhstan is Central Asia's largest economy and a major mining and oil/gas market.
The country's industrial base depends heavily on imported equipment and spares.
Chinese suppliers have a geographic advantage — shared border and established
trade corridors through Xinjiang and the China-Kazakhstan logistics hub.

## Buyer Profile

| Buyer Type | Description | Typical Need |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| Mining companies | Copper, coal, uranium, gold mining | Compressor parts, pump replacements, hydraulic spares |
| Oil & Gas operators | Tengiz, Kashagan, Karachaganak fields | Valve spares, filtration, compressor service |
| Industrial distributors | Almaty-based import/distribution companies | Multi-category industrial spares for resale |
| Service companies | Oilfield service, mining contractors | Wear parts, seals, hoses, tools |

## Key Industrial Keywords (Kazakhstan Market)

- `компрессор запчасти поставщик Китай` (compressor parts supplier China)

## Outreach Approach

1. **Russian-language content** — Primary business language; Kazakh secondary
2. **Almaty connections** — Most industrial distributors based in Almaty
3. **Cross-border logistics support** — Highlight China-Kazakhstan freight advantages
4. **Exhibition attendance** — Mining & Metals Central Asia — annual priority
5. **Sample delivery program** — Quick delivery via Khorgos dry port

## Language Requirements

- Primary business language: Russian
- Secondary: Kazakh (government and national company preference)
- English: Oil & Gas international operators
- Chinese: Cross-border logistics coordination

## Logistics Advantage

- Khorgos Gateway — China-Kazakhstan dry port, major transshipment hub
- Rail freight: 7-10 days from Urumqi to Almaty
- Truck freight: 3-5 days from Khorgos to Almaty
- Air freight: Urumqi to Almaty — daily flights

## Priority Systems for Kazakhstan

1. Air Compressor Systems — Mining operations primary demand
2. Pumps & Fluid Handling — Mine dewatering, oil transfer
3. Hydraulic Systems — Heavy mining equipment maintenance
4. Industrial Filtration — Mining dust collection, oil filtration
5. Valves & Flow Control — Oil & Gas pipeline applications

## Risk Notes

- Payment: USD and CNY both accepted; local banks have China correspondent relationships
- Certification: EAC/GOST-K certificate for industrial equipment
- Local content preferences: Some tenders require local partner or representative office
- Competition: Turkish and Russian suppliers also active; price + quality + logistics = advantage
