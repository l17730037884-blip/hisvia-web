# UAE Market Entry Plan

## Market Overview

The UAE is the Middle East's premier re-export and industrial hub. Dubai serves
as a gateway to GCC, Africa, and South Asian markets. Industrial spare parts
demand is driven by oil & gas operations, construction, manufacturing, and
the UAE's role as a regional distribution center.

## Buyer Profile

| Buyer Type | Description | Typical Need |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| Oil & Gas operators | ADNOC group, oilfield service companies | Compressor parts, valve spares, filtration, pump replacements |
| Industrial distributors | Dubai/JAFZA-based re-export traders | Multi-brand industrial spares for GCC and Africa |
| Construction companies | Major infrastructure developers | Construction equipment parts, compressor rentals |
| Manufacturing plants | Aluminum, steel, petrochemical | Hydraulic systems, automation components, filtration |
| Marine/Offshore | Oil rig supply, ship repair, port operations | Marine-grade pumps, valves, hydraulics |

## Outreach Approach

1. **ADIPEC attendance** — World's largest oil & gas exhibition (Abu Dhabi, November) — top priority
2. **Dubai distributor network** — Target JAFZA-based re-export companies
3. **English-language industrial website** — Full technical content optimization
4. **Arabic-language landing pages** — Key product categories, growing regional preference
5. **LinkedIn industrial marketing** — GCC procurement managers active on LinkedIn
6. **Sample readiness** — UAE buyers expect fast sample delivery before bulk orders

## Language Requirements

- Primary: English — GCC business language
- Secondary: Arabic — Government entities and some large industrial groups
- Technical specifications: English
- Commercial documents: English (standard practice)

## Logistics Advantage

- Sea freight: Major China-UAE shipping routes; 18-25 days to Jebel Ali Port
- Air freight: Daily cargo flights to Dubai and Abu Dhabi
- Jebel Ali Free Zone (JAFZA): Re-export hub for GCC, Africa, South Asia
- No import duties in Free Zones (for re-export)

## Priority Systems for UAE

1. Air Compressor Systems — Oil & Gas, construction, manufacturing
2. Valves & Flow Control — Oil & Gas pipeline and process applications
3. Industrial Filtration — Oil & Gas, desalination, manufacturing
4. Pumps & Fluid Handling — Oil transfer, desalination, construction
5. Hydraulic Systems — Marine, offshore, heavy equipment
6. Automation Systems — Manufacturing modernization, Industry 4.0

## Risk Notes

- Payment: USD standard; LC and T/T both common; payment terms 30-90 days typical
- Certification: ISO 9001 minimum; API, ASME for oil & gas products preferred
- Competition: Established European and Indian suppliers; Chinese advantage = price + capability range
- Relationship: UAE business is relationship-driven; in-person visits and trade show presence matter
- Re-export dynamics: Many buyers are re-exporters — packaging and documentation for onward shipping important
