# Uzbekistan Market Entry Plan

## Market Overview

Uzbekistan is Central Asia's most populous market (36M+) undergoing rapid
industrialization. Government programs are driving mining expansion (gold, copper,
uranium), oil & gas modernization, and manufacturing development. Industrial
equipment import demand is growing aggressively.

## Buyer Profile

| Buyer Type | Description | Typical Need |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| State mining enterprises | Navoi Mining (gold/uranium), Almalyk (copper) | Compressor parts, pump spares, hydraulic components |
| Oil & Gas operators | Uzbekneftegaz modernization projects | Valve spares, filtration, compressor service |
| Industrial distributors | Tashkent-based import/wholesale companies | Multi-system industrial spares |
| Construction companies | Infrastructure and industrial construction | Construction equipment parts, compressor rentals |
| Textile manufacturers | Growing textile industry | Compressed air systems, steam systems |

## Outreach Approach

1. **Dual-language content** — Russian for business, Uzbek growing in importance
2. **Tashkent focus** — Capital is the business hub; all major buyers headquartered here
3. **Government tender participation** — Many large buyers are state enterprises
4. **Local agent/partner** — Critical for navigating state enterprise procurement
5. **Sample and small trial orders** — Uzbek buyers prefer to test before bulk commitment

## Language Requirements

- Business and technical: Russian (primary)
- Government and national companies: Uzbek increasingly preferred
- English: Limited — not sufficient as primary business language
- RFQ forms should accept Russian and Uzbek inputs

## Logistics

- Rail: China-Kazakhstan-Uzbekistan corridor; 15-20 days
- Truck: Via Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan; 10-14 days
- Air: Tashkent International Airport from Urumqi

## Priority Systems for Uzbekistan

1. Air Compressor Systems — Mining, textile, construction
2. Pumps & Fluid Handling — Mining dewatering, irrigation modernization
3. Hydraulic Systems — Mining equipment maintenance
4. Industrial Filtration — Mining dust control, oil/gas filtration
5. Automation Systems — Manufacturing modernization programs

## Risk Notes

- Currency: UZS (Som) — USD pricing preferred; currency conversion may be restricted
- Payment: Letter of Credit common for state enterprises; T/T for private buyers
- Import procedures: Can be bureaucratic; local customs broker recommended
- Competition: Growing presence of Chinese, Turkish, and Russian suppliers
- Relationship-driven: In-person meetings and trust-building critical for state buyers
