# Russia Market Entry Plan

## Market Overview

Russia is the largest CIS market for industrial equipment spares and replacement parts.
After Western OEM supply chain disruptions, Chinese suppliers have become primary alternative
sources for compressor parts, hydraulic components, pumps, valves, and industrial filtration.

## Buyer Profile

| Buyer Type | Description | Typical Need |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| Mining companies | Coal, gold, diamond mining operations | Compressor parts, hydraulic cylinders, pump replacements |
| Oil & Gas operators | Upstream and midstream facilities | Valve spares, compressor service kits, filtration |
| Industrial distributors | Regional spare parts dealers | Multi-brand replacement parts inventory |
| Service/repair centers | Equipment maintenance companies | Wear parts, seals, filters, bearings |
| OEM dealers (alternative) | Dealers seeking cost-competitive sources | Aftermarket parts for Atlas Copco, IR, Kaeser |

## Key Industrial Keywords (Russian Market)

- `запчасти для компрессоров Китай` (compressor parts China)
- `гидравлические компоненты поставщик` (hydraulic components supplier)
- `промышленные фильтры производство Китай` (industrial filters China manufacturing)
- `замена насосов промышленных` (industrial pump replacement)
- `клапаны регулирующие поставки` (control valve supply)
- `Atlas Copco аналоги Китай` (Atlas Copco alternatives China)
- `Ingersoll Rand запчасти поставщик` (Ingersoll Rand parts supplier)

## Outreach Approach

1. **Russian-language website** — Full `/ru` localization with industrial terminology accuracy
2. **Yandex.Webmaster** — Site verification and indexing for Russian search
3. **Telegram B2B channel** — Regular posts on Chinese industrial supply capability
4. **Exhibition presence** — MiningWorld Russia (April, Moscow) — top priority
5. **Local representative** — Russian-speaking industrial specialist for buyer communication

## Language Requirements

- All buyer-facing content: Russian
- Technical specifications: Russian + English
- RFQ form: Russian interface, English backend
- Response time expectation: Same business day (MSK timezone)

## Market Intelligence Sources

- Russian industrial import statistics (customs data)
- Mining company procurement announcements
- Yandex keyword volume data
- Competitor analysis (Chinese suppliers already active in Russia)

## Priority Systems for Russia

1. Air Compressor Systems — Mining and oil/gas demand highest
2. Hydraulic Systems — Excavator and heavy machinery spares
3. Industrial Filtration — Compressor and hydraulic oil filtration
4. Pumps & Fluid Handling — Mine dewatering, oil transfer
5. Valves & Flow Control — Pipeline and process valves

## Risk Notes

- Payment routes evolving (non-SWIFT alternatives required)
- Logistics via rail (China-Russia) and sea (Far East ports)
- Certification requirements: EAC (Eurasian Conformity) for many industrial products
- Sanctions compliance: Verify end-user and end-use for dual-use items
