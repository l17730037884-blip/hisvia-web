## Key Findings

### Strengths
1. **Clear non-ecommerce positioning** — "What we are NOT" is strong and prominent
2. **Industrial language** — Uses real equipment terms, certification standards
3. **Sourcing scenarios** — Every system page has specific, actionable scenarios
4. **Structured partner program** — Onboarding steps and requirements are well-defined

### Gaps
1. **IP protection** — Critical for OEM buyers, completely missing
2. **Trust evidence** — No supplier verification process description
3. **Revenue model** — Partner pages lack commission/revenue structure
4. **Urgency** — No rush/expedited capability mentioned
5. **Social proof** — No customer logos, case references (even anonymized)

### Risk Items
- OEM buyers may leave due to IP concerns (HIGH)
- Distributors may view HISVIA as competitor not enabler (MEDIUM)
- No external trust signals beyond content claims (MEDIUM)
