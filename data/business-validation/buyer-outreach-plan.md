## Buyer Qualification Criteria

A buyer is marked as "qualified" when ALL of the following are met:

1. ✅ Real company verified (website, LinkedIn, or business registry)
2. ✅ Decision maker or influencer identified
3. ✅ Specific industrial need expressed (system type, brand, or application)
4. ✅ Budget or purchase timeline discussed
5. ✅ Contact information confirmed (email + phone)

## Outreach Sequence

### Day 1: Initial Contact
- LinkedIn connection request or email introduction
- Value proposition: "Chinese industrial supply chain partner for [their industry]"
- Call to action: Visit HISVIA website, check capability pages

### Day 3: Follow-up (if no response)
- Share relevant system page link (e.g., /solutions/compressors)
- Mention compatible brands they likely use

### Day 7: Second Follow-up
- Share a relevant case/problem scenario
- Ask: "Are you currently sourcing [product type] from China?"

### Day 14: Final Follow-up
- Offer: "We can help you find alternative suppliers for [brand] parts"
- If no response, mark as `lost` and move on

### Post-Response: Qualification
- Schedule call or video meeting
- Understand their requirement in detail
- Map to HISVIA system/category
- If qualified → send RFQ form
- If not → nurture (newsletter, occasional updates)
