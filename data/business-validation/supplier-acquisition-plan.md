## Selection Criteria (Minimum Requirements)

1. **Legal entity**: Valid Chinese business license (营业执照) — non-negotiable
2. **Export experience**: Minimum 2 years exporting industrial products — preferred
3. **Quality system**: ISO 9001 certified — minimum requirement
4. **Product fit**: Products match HISVIA taxonomy categories
5. **Communication**: At least one English or Russian-speaking staff member — strongly preferred
6. **Verification willingness**: Agrees to document verification process

## Outreach Template Framework

### Initial Contact (WeChat/Email)

Key points:
- Introduce HISVIA as industrial supply chain platform
- Explain value: connect Chinese manufacturing to overseas buyers
- No cost to factory for listing (clarify business model)
- Request: company profile, product catalog, certificates
- Next step: document submission for verification

### Follow-up (Day 7 after no response)

- Gentle reminder
- Attach HISVIA capability overview (1-page PDF)
- Mention target markets (Russia, Kazakhstan, UAE)
- Ask if they have export interest
